Sungmin Yun

Computer Architecture · Systems for AI

I am a Senior Deep Learning Performance Architect at NVIDIA, focusing on AI acceleration, performance analysis, and optimization. My work aims to maximize the performance and efficiency of AI systems through hardware–software co-design.

My experience spans accelerator design, hardware profiling, performance modeling, and kernel programming for modern AI architectures, including MoE, MLA, RAG, and diffusion. My research has focused on efficient LLM serving, Multi-LoRA inference, and RAG pipelines. I also developed LLMSimulator, an open-source end-to-end LLM performance simulator.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Seoul National University (SNU). I received my Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence from SNU, under the supervision of Prof. Jung Ho Ahn in the SCALE Lab. I received my B.S. in Integrated Information Technology from Yonsei University in 2020.
